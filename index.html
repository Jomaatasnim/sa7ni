        Help & Support

        How do I add a recipe?

        Open the Add page, fill in the title, ingredients and steps, then tap “Publish recipe”.

        How do I save a favorite?

        Open any recipe and tap “Add To Favorites”. Find it later in the Favorites page.

        How do I rate a recipe?

        Tap a star on the recipe page. Tap the same star again to remove your rating.

        Where is my data stored?

        Everything stays in this browser. Clearing the browser data deletes your recipes, favorites and ratings.
